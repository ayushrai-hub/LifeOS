# 🛠️ Tech-Arsenal: Comprehensive Learning Ecosystem

> **Transform engineers into Product-Oriented Systems Engineers with ML/AI Engineering Mastery**

This repository contains two educational platforms that develop full-stack, systems-savvy engineers with deep ML and AI engineering expertise. Graduates become **Product-Oriented Systems Engineers** who can build, deploy, and scale intelligent systems while maintaining platform reliability and security expertise.

## 📚 Educational Platforms

### 🎯 **Two Learning Pathways Available**

**🏗️ Tech-Arsenal (Original Curriculum)**
- Comprehensive modular curriculum with 9 thematic modules
- 45 sections covering technology fundamentals to advanced engineering
- Resource-rich learning with curated books, papers, and tools
- Perfect for methodic learning and technology exploration

**🚀 Karm (Advanced Learning Ecosystem)**
- Industry-leading comprehensive curriculum with rigorous assessment
- 10 modules, 44+ skill-testing frameworks, and measurable competencies
- Working code examples, automated assessment, and career development
- Complete portfolio system with project frameworks and career paths
- 2024-2025 current technology standards throughout

- **📁 curriculum/**: The complete learning curriculum organized in thematic modules

- **📋 docs/maintenance.md**: Comprehensive maintenance and contribution guide
