About

Indie Builder (Engineer) shipping products, services, then AI Engineer designing and shipping production LLM applications, RAG systems, and evaluation pipelines.

Ayush Rai is an AI Engineer based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. His public roles are AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, and LLM and Evaluation Engineer. He designs and ships production AI systems, with emphasis on LLM applications, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation pipelines, and automation. Backend work is grounded in Python and FastAPI. The work spans startups, enterprise clients, and frontier AI evaluation programs.

This website is the public reading view of a living knowledge system: career, projects, products, communities, domains, speaking, writing, fitness, and learning. It is maintained as static HTML from notes in the LifeOS repository. Pages describe what shipped, what broke, and what he would do differently. They do not invent metrics.

The primary engineering role recorded here is at FoCDoT Technologies. Evaluation and human-feedback programs associated with Scale AI, Turing, Snorkel AI, and Airdawgs Labs are one expertise area, not duplicate jobs. Flagship projects are Adaptive RAG, MailU, and the OpenAI-RLHF evaluation repository. Public products in progress are Shiksha-Mitra and RevenueRoles. Education includes a BTech in computer science and engineering and study toward the IIT Madras BS in Data Science. Public work uses English and Hindi.

He grew up in Seoni, Madhya Pradesh, where his family moved from a village so the children could get an English-medium education. The longer version is on the Story page.

Pune appears only as historical or work context where a source requires it. Street and residential addresses are not published. To talk, use the contact page.
