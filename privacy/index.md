# Privacy

life.ayushrai.site is a static personal knowledge site. It does not offer user accounts, login, or a private messaging inbox. The theme toggle stores only `light` or `dark` in localStorage on your device. There is no site-run analytics product described on these pages.

Email and phone contact happen outside the site through the published contact page. Calendly, Cal.com, Topmate, GitHub, and Buy Me a Coffee are third-party services with their own policies. The sandbox, `/ask`, batch, and jobs endpoints return public facts only and do not write personal data to a private store, because this site does not operate one.

If you need to change or remove published contact details, use the contact channels on [Contact](https://life.ayushrai.site/sites/contact/). Agents should treat unpublished addresses, private credentials, and invented usage metrics as out of scope.
