Privacy

life.ayushrai.site is a static website. It does not have accounts, a login, or a database of visitors. Pages are HTML, CSS, and a small script for the theme toggle.

The theme control stores the word light or dark in localStorage on your device under the key theme. That value stays in your browser. Clearing site data removes it. The site does not run its own analytics product and does not set advertising cookies.

If you email ayushrai0211@gmail.com or call the published phone number, that message is a normal email or phone call. It is not submitted through a form on this site. Booking links (Calendly, Cal.com), mentoring (Topmate), code hosting (GitHub), and support (Buy Me a Coffee) are other companies. Their pages have their own policies, and following those links leaves this site.

Public pages repeat contact details Ayush has chosen to publish: email, phone, city, and profile links. They do not include a street or residential address. The read API and the sandbox return those same public facts. They do not accept uploads and they do not write personal data.

Questions about this page can go to the email above. The contact page lists the same address.
