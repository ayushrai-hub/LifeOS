# Storytelling craft

## Arc I practice

Hook → stakes → complication → insight → concrete ask.

## Rules

- One idea per talk or essay.
- Show a real failure or constraint when possible.
- Never invent audience size, titles, or outcomes.
- After each talk: what landed, what dragged, what to cut.
