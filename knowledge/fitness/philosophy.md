# Fitness philosophy

Fitness is baseline capacity for thinking and building, not a separate persona.

## Track over years

Body weight, measurements, gym journey, strength milestones, nutrition, running, MMA, health metrics, progress photos, lessons, goals.

## Rules

- Prefer consistency logs over heroic weeks.
- Photos are entities with dates (`media/photos/` + `fitness/photos/` links).
- Milestones go in `fitness/milestones/`; weekly/period logs in `fitness/logs/`.
