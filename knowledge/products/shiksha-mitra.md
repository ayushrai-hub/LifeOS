# Shiksha-Mitra

## Problem

Personal growth is often solitary. The product frames paired accountability (“Mitra”) so two people share check-ins, goals, and support.

## Who it serves

People seeking a growth companion rather than a casual chat app; education-adjacent mutual accountability.

## Current state

**Building.** Live marketing / app surface on Vercel; public GitHub repo with React/Vite TypeScript frontend (landing sections, dashboard feature UIs). Backend pairing infrastructure is not claimed here beyond what the repo and live site show.
