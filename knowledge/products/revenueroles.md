# RevenueRoles

## Problem

Technical revenue roles (GTM Engineer, FDE, RevOps, AI Sales Engineer, etc.) are mislabeled or late on general job boards. The product positions a specialized taxonomy and discovery layer for those roles.

## Who it serves

Candidates hunting technical-revenue roles, and recruiters hiring for those titles.

## Current state

**Building** (site labels Alpha 0.9). Live product: [revenueroles.in](https://revenueroles.in/). Source monorepo with web app under `apps/web`.
