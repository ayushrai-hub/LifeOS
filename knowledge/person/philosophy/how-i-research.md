# How I Research

Research here means disciplined investigation, not an academic title.

## Pattern

- Write the question before collecting links.
- Separate primary sources from commentary.
- Prefer reproducible experiments when studying systems.
- Publish findings as `research/` or `thinking/` notes with confidence labels.

## Growing edge

Literature notes; cleaner evaluation datasets for AI systems I build.
