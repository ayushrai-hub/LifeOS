# How I Design Systems

This knowledge base is the training ground: typed entities, stable IDs, relationships, archive instead of delete.

## Principles

- One fact, one place.
- Add folders and entities; avoid restructuring for fashion.
- Consumers (site, AI, search) stay replaceable.
- Design for failure: drafts, review, provenance.

## Growing edge

Richer graph queries; media metadata discipline; automated gallery generation later.
