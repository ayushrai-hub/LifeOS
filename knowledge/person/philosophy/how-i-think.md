# How I Think

I try to keep three layers distinct: what I saw, what I believe, and how sure I am.

## Habits

- Prefer mechanisms over slogans.
- Ask what would change my mind.
- Hold geopolitics and market takes as provisional until notes and sources exist.
- Use storytelling to stress-test clarity, not to decorate weak ideas.

## Growing edge

Faster detection of motivated reasoning; better second-order thinking on incentives and power.
