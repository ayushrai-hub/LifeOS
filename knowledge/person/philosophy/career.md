# Career Philosophy

Career is a portfolio of shipped systems, honest learning stages, and people helped, recorded here so memory cannot rewrite it.

## Operating pattern

1. Take roles that expand real capability (AI systems, product, policy literacy).
2. Keep parallel crafts alive: speaking, storytelling, markets, geopolitics.
3. Mentoring and writing as tests of understanding.
4. Prefer durable reputation over temporary title inflation.

## What I refuse

- CV fiction.
- Collecting logos without transferable skill.
- Letting any single employer become the whole identity.

## Growing edge

Clearer narrative of impact with evidence; selective depth amid many trails.
