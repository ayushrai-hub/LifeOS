# How I Approach Business

## Stance

A project is not a business until someone gets value repeatedly and a path to sustain it exists.

## Habits

- Write market notes before large builds (`domain.market-understanding`).
- Track alternatives and switching costs.
- Use `ideas/` with kill criteria.
- Read geopolitics and economics as context for tech bets, not as hot takes.

## Growing edge

Customer conversations; pricing experiments; clearer positioning language.
