# Writing Philosophy

I write to pin down what I actually believe after contact with a system — not to perform expertise.

## Operating pattern

1. Capture raw notes in `notes/` or `thinking/`.
2. Promote durable pieces to `writing/`.
3. One idea per piece; show a constraint or failure when possible.
4. Link domains, projects, and technologies by `id`.

## What I refuse

- SEO filler.
- Invented case-study metrics.
- Ghostwritten voice that is not mine.

## Growing edge

Regular public pieces; tighter storytelling craft (`speaking/storytelling/craft.md`).
