# How I Prioritize

I want fewer open loops and more finished learning cycles.

## Rules of thumb

- Active domains beat spectator domains.
- Work that produces an entity (project, note, talk, impact evidence) beats work that only feels busy.
- Fitness and health stay non-negotiable baseline capacity.
- Novelty is a tax unless it unlocks a goal.

## Growing edge

Saying no earlier; parking ideas in `ideas/` instead of half-building them.
