# How I Write

Also see `philosophy.writing`.

## Pattern

- Draft privately in `notes/` or `thinking/`.
- Promote durable pieces into `writing/` with title, summary, date, tags, related entities.
- Prefer specific verbs and measured claims.
- Let storytelling structure long pieces without theatrical language.

## Growing edge

Publishing cadence; editorial passes; linking every article into domains and projects.
