# How I Communicate

Say the true thing simply. Use story to carry structure, not to hide gaps.

## Habits

- Lead with the claim, then evidence.
- For talks: hook → tension → insight → ask.
- For writing: summary before depth.
- Calibrate confidence in language (`VOICE_AND_TONE.md`).

## Growing edge

Public speaking reps; clearer non-technical explanations; tighter slides.
