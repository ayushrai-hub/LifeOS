# AI Philosophy

I treat AI as infrastructure under constraints (latency, cost, reliability, safety), not as magic slides.

## Operating pattern

1. Define the job-to-be-done and failure modes first.
2. Prefer retrieval, tools, and evaluation over blind model worship.
3. Ship the smallest loop that can be measured.
4. Write what broke before claiming “production.”

## What I refuse

- Inflating model names into personal identity.
- Publishing accuracy claims without a harness or honest caveats.
- Confusing partner-program exposure with sole authorship of foundation models.

## Growing edge

Stronger evals, better agent orchestration discipline, and clearer product wedges (`thinking/market-lens.md`).
