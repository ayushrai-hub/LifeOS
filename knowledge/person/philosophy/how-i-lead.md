# How I Lead

Leadership for me starts before a title: set direction, own outcomes, credit others, and do not bluff.

## Habits

- Make the decision criteria visible.
- Prefer small accountable scopes.
- Log real decisions in `leadership/` when they happen.

## Growing edge

More reps coordinating people; fewer silent assumptions.
