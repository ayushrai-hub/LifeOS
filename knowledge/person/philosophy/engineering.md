# Engineering Philosophy

I engineer for reality: interfaces, failure, and maintenance, not for cleverness alone.

## Operating pattern

1. Clarify the constraint (time, correctness, scale).
2. Design the thinnest architecture that can evolve.
3. Instrument early; debug with evidence.
4. Leave the next engineer (often future-me) a map.

## What I refuse

- Premature platforms.
- Undocumented “it works on my machine” heroes.
- Rewriting for fashion when append/archive would do (same rule as this PKB).

## Growing edge

Deeper systems design practice; cleaner handoffs between AI components and classical backends.
