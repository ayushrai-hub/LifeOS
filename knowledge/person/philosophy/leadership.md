# Leadership Philosophy

Leadership is making progress legible and decisions reversible when possible. Titles without ownership are empty.

## Operating pattern

1. Name the goal and the constraint.
2. Assign clear owners; remove ambiguity.
3. Review work with standards, not vibes.
4. Protect focus; escalate blockers early.

## What I refuse

- Status meetings that replace shipping.
- Claiming team outcomes as solo heroics.
- Softening bad news until it becomes a crisis.
