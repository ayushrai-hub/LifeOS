# Geopolitics lens (standing note)

## Observation

The tools I use (models, chips, clouds, data regimes) sit inside contested national and corporate systems.

## Current view

I follow geopolitics to avoid naive product and career bets: compute access, data residency, export controls, platform risk. I write notes; I do not perform certainty.

## What would change my mind

Primary sources and outcomes that falsify a trend I was tracking; then I revise this note and any linked principles.

## Promote to principle?

Maybe after a year of dated notes.
