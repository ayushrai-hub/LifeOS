# Market lens (standing note)

## Observation

Shipping code is easy to confuse with creating a market position.

## Current view

Before a large build I want three sentences: who hurts, what they do today, why they would switch. If I cannot write them, the idea stays in `ideas/` as seed.

## What would change my mind

Repeated user conversations that contradict my wedge; or a competitor that already owns the outcome with a better distribution path.

## Promote to principle?

Maybe → refine after a few product cycles.
