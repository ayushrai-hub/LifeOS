# Interpretable analytics

## What is it?

Scoring and ranking methods where the decision rule is inspectable (equations, transparent features) rather than opaque model fits; especially when stakeholders demand explanations.

## Why it matters

In credit/card profitability and regulated decisions, black boxes can be disallowed or untrusted. Interpretability is sometimes a **constraint**, not a preference.
