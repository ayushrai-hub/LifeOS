# Speak to land ideas

## Statement

Practice speaking and storytelling as engineering skills: structure, stakes, one ask.

## Why it exists

Technical work fails in silence; persuasion without substance also fails. Train both.

## Examples in practice

`speaking/storytelling/craft.md` arc; open mic as early stage practice; mentoring as forced clarity.

## Exceptions

Not every note needs a stage, but every major project should get a human-readable story.
