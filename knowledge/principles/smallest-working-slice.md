# Smallest working slice

## Statement

Prefer a measurable slice over a beautiful unfinished system.

## Why it exists

Premature architecture hides whether the problem was real.

## Examples in practice

How-I-learn and how-I-build checklists; market-lens three sentences before large builds.

## Exceptions

Safety-critical paths may need more upfront design; still time-box and document assumptions.
