# Evidence over titles

## Statement

If I cannot point to evidence, I mark `draft`/`review`; I do not invent polish.

## Why it exists

Portfolio culture rewards slogans. This knowledge base exists to resist that.

## Examples in practice

- Impact ledger starts empty until proof exists.
- Certifications without issuer stay `review`.
- Open mic honor kept without invented venue/date.

## Exceptions

Private drafts may be speculative; published surfaces must not be.
