## Page contract

Every page should include:

1. `<!DOCTYPE html>`, `lang="en"`
2. `viewport` meta
3. Unique `<title>` and `meta description`
4. Skip link to `#content`
5. Semantic `<main>`
6. Shared `styles/site.css`
7. No em dashes
