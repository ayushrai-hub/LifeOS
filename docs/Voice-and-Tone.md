# Voice and Tone

## Voice (stable)

The voice is:

- precise
- calm
- curious
- evidence-oriented
- willing to admit uncertainty and failure
- allergic to hype

It should sound like a careful engineer writing for other careful people; not a personal brand coach.

## Tone (adapts slightly by surface)

| Surface | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Root homepage | Extremely spare; almost only names and links |
| Case studies | Analytical, structured, tradeoff-aware |
| Research | Cautious; distinguish claim vs hypothesis |
| Notes | Informal, incomplete OK, dated |
| Career / resume | Factual, scannable, metric-backed |
| Leadership / management | Concrete behaviors and decisions, not slogans |
| Failures | Direct, non-dramatic, focused on lessons |
| Blog / essays | Clear narrative allowed; still no fluff |

## Personality without performance

Reveal the human through:

- failures and corrections
- decision records
- values stated as operating rules
- how problems were prioritized
- what was refused and why

Do not perform personality with jokes in every paragraph, emoji storms, or forced quirkiness.

Light dry humor is acceptable if it does not obscure meaning.

## Confidence calibration

| Situation | Language |
|-----------|----------|
| Measured result | State the number and method |
| Strong belief, weak evidence | “I currently believe… because…” |
| Unknown | “I do not know yet.” |
| Team achievement | Credit the team; specify your role |

## Words that usually signal bad tone

Delete or rewrite:

- excited to share
- thrilled
- game-changing
- unlock
- elevate
- empower
- seamless
- robust (unless comparing failure modes)
- scalable (unless stating scale numbers)

## Consistency across subdomains

Voice stays the same everywhere. A notes page can be rougher in finish, but not different in character; still honest, still specific.
